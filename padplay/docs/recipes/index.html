PadPlay

Guides

Common things to build: what to add, what to set and the script.

  • Steer by turning the phone — Turn a phone held sideways into a steering value from -1 to 1.
  • Give each player a private map — Send each player's own camera to their phone only.
  • Aim with the gyro and shoot — A crosshair follows the phone; press A to shoot.
  • Point phones at on-screen menus — A cursor follows each phone; press A to click the uGUI button under it. Several players at once.
  • Use the phone like a zoom lens — The phone turns a camera whose picture it shows: hold it up to look around, hold A to zoom.
  • Two or more players — Spawn a character when a phone joins; remove it when the player is really gone.
