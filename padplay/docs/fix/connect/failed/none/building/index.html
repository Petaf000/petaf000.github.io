PadPlay

Phones cannot connect

The Wi-Fi keeps devices from reaching each other

  1. Common with apartment, school, office and hotel Wi-Fi. PadPlay can't change it.
  2. Connect the PC to the phone's hotspot, or turn on Windows' Mobile hotspot and connect the phone to it.
  3. The QR code changes when you switch, so scan the new one.
  4. Newer app versions listen for the PC's notice (Announce On LAN) to tell whether the PC or the Wi-Fi stops them, and then say "This Wi-Fi blocks the connection".
