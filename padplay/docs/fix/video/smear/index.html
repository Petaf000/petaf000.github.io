PadPlay

Picture is black, broken or slow

The picture is being repaired

  1. Newer app versions compare their picture with the PC's every few seconds and ask for a key frame when they differ. Players see no message.
  2. The repairs are counted in host.RequestVideoStats (PictureRecoveries) and in PadPlay Debug's report.
  3. On a PC where the picture keeps breaking after repairs, the SDK switches to readback by itself (the same slower but reliable path as Prefer Readback). The reason shows in PadPlay Debug's video status and report. To stop it, turn off Host › Video › Auto Prefer Readback.

  • AutoPreferReadback
