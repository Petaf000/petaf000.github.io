PadPlay

Coming soon

A Unity SDK that turns phones into second-screen controllers.

Players open the PadPlay app and scan a QR code, and their phone joins your game. Each phone can be a touch panel, a gyro pointer, a gamepad with vibration, and a second screen that shows that player’s own picture.

PadPlay SDK
Everything, including second-screen video. Comes with the source of the phone app, so you can publish it under your own name.
PadPlay Lite
The same players, touch, gyro, buttons and vibration, without video.
